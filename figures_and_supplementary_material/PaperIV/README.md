# Paper IV figures and frozen result summaries

Main Figures 1–4, SI Figures S1–S3, and Figure Zero are byte-extracted from the clean manuscripts in `manuscript/PaperIV/` or copied from the standalone TOC artwork. The rendered figures are publication assets. The `Q6_results/` directory is the frozen registered QM analysis (80/80 QC PASS, positive overall score, strict registered FAIL: N–Cα, C–O and C–N(next) worsened after Holm correction). `M2E_pilot/` reports a descriptive 1 ns GB3/ubiquitin pilot (+0.125966 Hz² equal-protein paired MSE contrast; five of six pairs favorable), with no Gate M pass or inferential claim. `section22_k80/` documents the corrected protein strain/bootstrap and temporal holdout (39.6%, CI 39.0–40.1%; blind 40.6% vs training 38.7%).

Source primary PDFs and raw MD trajectories are omitted from this public update; the M2E input manifest documents their identities. The Q6 analysis script needs original frozen Q5 input files for full recomputation. The publication-ready composite Figure 4 was extracted from the manuscript. Historical DNA plotting scripts have a stale BII interpretation and were deliberately excluded from this public staging package; audit and update the existing repository DNA pipeline before declaring that panel independently reproducible. Do not infer that all experiments can be reproduced from this small repository directory without their frozen input datasets.
