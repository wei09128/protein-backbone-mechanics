INTERPRETATION GUIDE

  Class analysis:
   • If B (branched) bar differs significantly from U (unbranched)
     in most basins → β-branching is a major modulator.
   • If Ar bar differs too → aromatic sidechains act similarly.
   • If G bar (GLY, no Cβ) is flat → Cβ is necessary for the effect.

  Rotamer analysis:
   • If g⁻, trans, g⁺ bars differ significantly within a basin
     → the sidechain is mechanically transmitting force through Cα.
   • If bars are identical → sidechain orientation doesn't matter,
     so the 'lever' story is too simple.
